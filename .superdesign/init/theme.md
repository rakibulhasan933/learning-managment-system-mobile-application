## Typography

- Display: Georgia/serif, large editorial headings
- UI: platform sans-serif
- Body: 14px with 21px line height
- Eyebrow: 11px brass uppercase-like tracking

## Shape and layout

- Dark ink background with elevated charcoal cards
- Rounded cards, generally 18–20px radius
- Pill buttons and badges
- 20px page horizontal padding
- Brass is the primary action/accent; teal indicates completion/offline success
